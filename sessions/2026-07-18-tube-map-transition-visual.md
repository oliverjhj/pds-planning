**Decisions made:**

- **The at-stop rule is index-relative, not gate-tag-relative** — `atStopIndexFor` fills the gate's stop only when it sits within ±1 of `current_stop_index`. A tag-based rule (`AnnounceOnDepart` = at a stop) was designed first and **refuted in plan review**: `StopDetectionInterpreter.kt:295` deliberately sets `SilentReArm` on terminus arrival to stop the detector re-firing while parked, and `JourneyCompletionController.kt:145` holds 10 s while the regulated overlay clears at ~8.5 s — so the band is exposed for ~1.5 s straight after the termination announcement and would have read "in transit to the terminus". The ±1 window fixes both terminus paths, resolves the origin-priming and mid-route-start offsets, rejects an arbitrary `jumpTo` and a diversion skip-walked landing, and **structurally guarantees the filled node is adjacent to `current`**, so it can never land outside the FR-AT-21 window view or in the dimmed passed region.
- **No animation on the tube map** (owner-approved deviation from the original sketch, which asked for "light moving along dashed lines"). Dashes were unavailable outright — Rule 20 reserves them for hail-and-ride semantics. Beyond that, a pulse would have been the first perpetual animation on the passenger surface, where motion is already allocated to the regulated flash and the amber warning markers. `ACTIVE_SEGMENT_STROKE_DP` is the salience lever instead, and the `build3-gps` TODO was retargeted at a real ~30 s position tick rather than a decorative loop.
- **No separate "at the stop" radius.** A tighter 5–10 m geofence was proposed and rejected: Rule 6 discards fixes whose accuracy exceeds the radius, and real bus GPS accuracy is commonly 10–30 m, so it would have discarded nearly every fix and failed exactly when the bus was at the stop. It would also have disagreed with both the announcement (which fires on exiting the stop's own radius) and the hero. If the fill ever reads too early, the lever is that stop's dashboard-authorable `proximity_radius_meters`.
- **Exactly one element is highlighted, and it is always the live position** (owner glass call, `aaf6988`). The first build gave the next stop a hollow outlined ring; that read as clutter, competing with the at-stop disc. The next stop now renders as a plain upcoming node and only lights up on arrival, identified meanwhile by its bold label and the highlighted segment running into it.
- **"Passed" keys off a reached frontier, not `current_stop_index`** (owner glass call, `dc7a8c3`). `current` names the stop being *approached* and runs one ahead of a standing bus, so the original rule dimmed the segment out of the bus's own stop at journey start and after a manual advance. An explicit `reachedIndex` (at-stop node when standing, last departed stop when moving) now divides driven from undriven track, so nothing ahead of the bus can carry the passed grey.
- **`DetectionEffects` gains no third index.** Ledger #31 made `gpsTerminusArrival` a boolean specifically to keep Rule 18's announced-vs-approached framing intact; the derivation lives outside, in `atStopIndexFor`.
- **The journey UI combines rather than passes through.** The gate moves synchronously inside the detection critical section while `current_stop_index` moves asynchronously via Room, so independent collection would transiently render the exact mismatched pair the feature exists to distinguish. `TubeMapUiState` closes that window — a deliberate departure from the Pattern-2 verbatim passthrough used for the monitors.
